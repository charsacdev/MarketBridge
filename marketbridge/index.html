Questions

Straight answers

The things people actually ask before signing up.

Does MarketBridge hold my money?
No, and it is built so that it cannot. There is no wallet and no balance in our system. Your funds stay in your Deriv account, trades execute there, and the balance you see here is a timestamped read of Deriv — not a number we maintain. Deposits and withdrawals hand you off to Deriv or to Kaastro, and we tell you clearly when you are leaving.
Is it free?
Yes. Creating an account, following traders, reading signals, joining market rooms and taking the courses all cost nothing. MarketBridge earns through Deriv's official partner programme rather than by charging traders for access.
Do you guarantee the signals?
No. Signals and analysis are written by other users, not by MarketBridge. We do not verify that any trade will work, and a boosted signal means we gave a post more visibility — never that we endorse the outcome. What we do guarantee is that the track record you see is honest: signals lock on publish, results resolve from evidence, and retracted signals still count against the author.
Which contracts can I actually trade here?
Accumulators, Rise/Fall, Digits and Multipliers on supported synthetic indices, plus others as Deriv's API allows. The platform checks what is genuinely executable for each market before it shows you a trade button — so if you see one, it works. Anything Deriv cannot execute from here is presented as analysis and points you to the right place instead.
Do I need a Deriv account?
To trade, yes. You can browse markets, read signals and take part in the community without one. When you are ready to trade, connect an existing Deriv account or create one from the connect screen.
Is my Deriv login safe?
We never see it. Connecting uses Deriv's own OAuth flow: you sign in on Deriv's site and grant specific permissions. We store an encrypted access token, never your password, and you can revoke access from your settings or from Deriv at any time.

Still unsure about something? Ask us directly — a person answers.
